COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Aurelius Protective Services in Western Australia.

Aurelius Protective Services operates under Western Australia Security Master Licence #74102, issued and regulated by the WA Police Licensing Services division under the Security and Related Activities (Control) Act 1996. Every individual security officer and crowd controller deployed on your site holds a current, valid WA security licence.

For planned operations and ongoing contracts, standard deployment is within 24 to 48 hours following site assessment and post orders sign-off. For emergency or urgent short-notice requirements in the Perth metropolitan area, our rapid-dispatch capability can deploy licensed officers within 2 to 4 hours.

Our primary operations cover the entire Perth Metropolitan Area (CBD, North, South, and Eastern corridors), Greater Fremantle, Joondalup, Mandurah, and Rockingham. We also provide dedicated security deployments and shutdown teams for regional WA resource, mining, and industrial sites across the Pilbara, Goldfields, and South West.

We maintain comprehensive, tier-1 insurance coverage including $20,000,000 Public and Products Liability Insurance, full statutory Workers' Compensation across all active personnel, and Professional Indemnity. Certificates of Currency are provided directly to clients during onboarding.

We maintain recruitment standards exceeding the industry baseline. All personnel undergo National Police History checks, licence verification with WA Police, scenario-based de-escalation testing, and site-specific inductions. Furthermore, our operations team conducts randomised supervisory audits and provides digital shift reports for 100% transparency.

Every shift produces a comprehensive digital report with verified GPS timestamps, access logs, visitor numbers, maintenance notes, and photograph-supported incident reports. Clients receive automated end-of-shift summaries and have 24/7 direct access to our senior Perth operations management.

You can request a quote by completing our online assessment form on this website, emailing operations@aureliusservices.com.au, or calling our Perth operations desk directly. We will review your site risk profile and provide a transparent, itemised proposal within 24 business hours.
