SCOPE & METHODOLOGIES

Targeted Protection for Critical Assets

Asset protection is distinct from general guarding. It focuses on specific, identified risks to high-value items, sensitive operations, or vulnerable locations. Every engagement begins with a threat assessment that maps potential vectors of loss, theft, damage, or compromise.

Our officers are deployed with specific standing orders designed for that asset, backed by GPS tracking, tamper-evident protocols, and incident escalation channels across Western Australia.
