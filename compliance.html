REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

Licensed Under Western Australia's Security Legislation

The security industry in Western Australia is strictly regulated under the Security and Related Activities (Control) Act 1996 and the Security and Related Activities (Control) Regulations 1997, administered by the WA Police Force Security Licensing Services.

Aurelius Security Services holds all required master and agent licences to operate as a security contractor throughout Western Australia. We guarantee that every deployed guard is fully licensed, insured, and verified.

Licence & Statutory Details

  • 100% WA License Compliant
    Authorised and compliant under WA Police Force Licensing Services, authorizing static guarding, crowd control, and patrol operations.
  • 100% Individual Guard Licences
    Every deployed officer holds an active individual licence endorsed for their exact scope of duties. Zero unvetted staff.
  • Statutory & Legislative Alignment
    Full compliance with the Security and Related Activities Act 1996, Liquor Control Act 1988, and WHS Act 2020 (WA).
