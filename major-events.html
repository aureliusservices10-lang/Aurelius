EVENT CHALLENGES

Scale, Complexity, and Risk Management

Major events present multifaceted risk environments: crowd density, multiple access points, alcohol and substance use, weather exposure, high-profile attendees, and complex compliance obligations with local councils, WA Police, and health authorities.

Aurelius coordinates directly with event organizers, venues, and emergency authorities to ensure full regulatory compliance, thorough risk mitigation, and seamless attendee flow under WA Licence #74102.

Event Operations Standards

  • Pre-Event Operations Planning
    Comprehensive risk management plans, crowd density modeling, and ingress/egress mapping.
  • Multi-Agency Coordination
    Liaison with WA Police, St John Ambulance, DFES, local councils, and liquor licensing.
  • On-Site Command & Control
    Dedicated security operations manager on site with radio communications and digital logging.

Access Control & Screening

High-volume entry processing: ticket verification, bag checks, metal detection, prohibited item confiscation, and age verification.

Crowd Flow & Zone Control

Managing movements between stages, food/beverage areas, and exits; preventing overcrowding; maintaining clear emergency paths.

VIP & Backstage Protection

Specialised security details for performers, keynote speakers, dignitaries, and backstage credential verification.

Emergency Coordination

Trained personnel ready for medical incidents, severe weather responses, evacuation management, and emergency service liaison.
