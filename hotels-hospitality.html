HOSPITALITY RISKS

Protecting the Atmosphere While Managing the Risk

Hospitality environments operate in a dynamic risk environment: alcohol service, large crowds, late operating hours, cash transactions, and diverse patron demographics.

Aurelius officers are trained to read room dynamics, anticipate problems before they escalate, and resolve situations with professional discretion — preserving the atmosphere while protecting your venue licence.

Operational Standards

  • Responsible Service of Alcohol (RSA) Support
    Active monitoring of intoxication levels and calm refusal of service.
  • Liquor Licensing Compliance
    Ensuring operations comply with WA Liquor Control Act requirements at all times.
  • Discreet Incident Resolution
    Managing unruly patrons without drawing unnecessary attention or disrupting other guests.

Hotel Lobby & Concierge

Guest Floor Patrols
