The 3 Core B2B Cleaning Verticals

Specialized facility sanitation engineered specifically for operational sensitivity, strict physical chain of custody, and corporate presentation in Western Australia.

VERTICAL 01

Corporate & CBD Offices

Executive presentation, touchpoint disinfection, boardrooms, client-facing suites, and data room sanitization.

KEY SERVICE SCOPE:

  • High-touchpoint virucidal disinfection across open-plan desks, lift banks, and shared amenities.
  • Executive boardroom, lounge, and client suite detailed detailing matching Tier-1 aesthetic standards.
  • Anti-static, residue-free micro-cleaning for IT server racks, comms rooms, and cable underflooring.
  • Secure shredding transfer & document confidentiality handling under signed corporate NDAs.
  • Unobtrusive evening or early morning schedules with zero disruption to corporate trading hours.
VERTICAL 02

Construction Handovers

White Card certified, fine silica extraction, builders final detail, and pre-occupancy compliance certificates.

KEY SERVICE SCOPE:

  • 100% White Card certified cleaning personnel operating in strict compliance with site PPE and safety inductions.
  • Industrial M-Class/H-Class HEPA extraction targeting airborne silica particulate on concrete, blockwork, and drywall.
  • Razor-safe architectural glazing polish, window track extraction, paint overspray, and mastic residue removal.
  • Defect identification logs and final hygiene sign-off ready for practical completion audits and tenant handovers.
  • High-access detailing for exposed interior ducting, cable trays, structural rafters, and architectural lighting.
VERTICAL 03

Industrial & Logistics

Workshop floor degreasing, high-pressure washdowns, machinery bay maintenance, and hazardous containment.

KEY SERVICE SCOPE:

  • Heavy-duty ride-on scrubbing and chemical concrete degreasing for forklift lanes, oils, and tire deposits.
  • High-pressure 3000+ PSI hot-water washdowns for heavy vehicle dispatch bays, loading docks, and hardstands.
  • Machinery perimeter maintenance complying with AS 4024 machine safety and lock-out tag-out (LOTO) protocols.
  • Spill kit replenishment and EPA-compliant trade waste management to prevent storm-water drainage contamination.
  • Compound perimeter upkeep, automated gatehouse sanitization, and security fence-line waste clearance.
